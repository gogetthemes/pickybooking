Free vs Pro

PickyBooking is a complete free plugin for WordPress.org. Pro is a separate commercial product with payments, integrations, and builder convenience features — not locked trialware inside Lite.

Lite = WP.org Pro = commercial

Why this split

  • Lite — minimum needed to design a map, take bookings, and get email alerts.
  • Pro — payments, CRM/automation, decorative polish, AI, and migration tools that save time at scale.

WordPress.org does not allow trialware (features present but locked behind payment). Lite simply omits Pro capabilities; Pro is distributed separately.

Installing both

Do not activate Lite and Pro together — they share post types and options. Use one edition per site.
